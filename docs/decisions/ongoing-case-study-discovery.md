# Ongoing case-study discovery

## Decision

Publish an incomplete story as `project_status: ongoing` with no completion month. Show it on `/case-studies/` as a sibling link list under In progress. Keep it out of the completed card grid.

Completed work remains in one collection. The retired Other Work split stays empty, and category filters and search remain deferred.

## Why this shape

A person should be able to find the story from the case-studies page. The article URL and the sitemap already include every published slug. The sibling links make ongoing stories discoverable while preserving the twelve completed stories, the first six, Load more, and the three homepage features.

## Alternatives

Article only. The story stays on its URL and in the sitemap, with an In progress line on the article. Visitors of `/case-studies/` cannot find it. That misses the reason for publishing it.

Second card grid. It would make prototypes discoverable, but it would also require changes to collection checks and browsing behavior. The existing card checks require completion dates. Simple sibling links keep that contract intact.

## Authorization

On 11 October 2026 the owner authorized worthwhile GitHub projects, including incomplete ones. The work may be unfinished. Do not invent a completion month or mark the story completed.
