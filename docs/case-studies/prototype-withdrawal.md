# Prototype withdrawal

On 11 October 2026, the owner requested removal of the public **In progress**
section and its three entries:

- Python bookkeeping reconciliation to Excel
- Single-camera padel video analysis prototype
- PyTorch image segmentation with a shared encoder

The three publication records are now draft tombstones. They are excluded from
the public collection, homepage selection, article lookup and sitemap. Their
nine public source images and display derivatives were removed, along with
their derivative registry entries. Historical review documents remain as
records of the earlier decision and link here for the current publication state.

The twelve existing completed case studies and their publication approvals are
unchanged. The collection omits its In progress section and jump link when the
ongoing list is empty.

Verification passed: 111 focused tests, a production build with 21 static
routes and 39 checked public links, and four browser checks covering the
collection plus all three withdrawn URLs in desktop and mobile Chromium and
WebKit. A before/after comparison confirmed that all twelve remaining public
stories and their approval metadata are unchanged.
