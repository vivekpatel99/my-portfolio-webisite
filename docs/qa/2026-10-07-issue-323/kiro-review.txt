- **Evidence:** The root error screen in `src/components/ErrorBoundary.jsx:17-22` is `<p>Something went wrong.{' '}<a>Back to home</a></p>`. With `exact: true`, Playwright compares the element's whole text, which here is "Something went wrong. Back to home". It never matches, so this line passes whether or not the app crashed.
- **Impact:** Low. A root crash replaces the whole app, so the header, `#main-content` and `#site-footer` checks on lines 23-25 still fail against the baseline. The spec still catches the bug.
